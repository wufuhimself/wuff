Your league's own history knows more than a national ranking site.

WuFF reads your Sleeper or ESPN league — rosters, drafts, standings, matchups — and builds keeper boards, mock drafts, and draft analysis from what your managers actually do, not a generic consensus.

What's live today

Connect a Sleeper league by username, or an ESPN league by ID — both self-serve, no waiting on approval. (Yahoo leagues are supported but currently added by request while Yahoo's API access is pending.)

Keeper board — click, and watch the draft board move

Every keeper-eligible player, scored and ranked by this league's own rules — round restrictions, consecutive-season caps. Click a card to mark a keeper or drop one; the remaining draft board reorders live, so you can try out different keeper calls before committing and see exactly how each one reshapes the board.

It also shows position scarcity as keepers lock in — this league's board right now has 11 of the top 20 WRs already kept, so WR depth on draft day is thinner than a generic ranking site would suggest. That's the kind of thing only your own league's data can tell you.

Mock draft

Simulates your league's draft from current keepers and market rankings, with position needs and team tendencies factored in — not just best-player-available.

Draft patterns & analysis

What this league actually drafts and when, straight from its own draft history — and whether draft position has predicted final standings.

Manager report card

Grades each manager's draft performance against this league's own baseline — did they finish better or worse than their draft slot predicted.

Matchups & playoffs

Weekly head-to-head scores and the live playoff bracket, updated as games are played.

Standings & draft history

Full season standings and every past draft, in one place per league.
