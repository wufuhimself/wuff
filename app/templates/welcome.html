Every keeper-eligible player, scored and ranked by this league's own rules — round restrictions, consecutive-season caps. Click a card to mark a keeper or drop one; the remaining draft board reorders live, so you can try out different keeper calls before committing and see exactly how each one reshapes the board.

It also shows position scarcity as keepers lock in — this league's board right now has 11 of the top 20 WRs already kept, so WR depth on draft day is thinner than a generic ranking site would suggest. That's the kind of thing only your own league's data can tell you.
